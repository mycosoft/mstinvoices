# Product Mission (Lite)

Invoice Generator is a Laravel-based company management platform for tech service companies to manage quotes, invoices, expenses, and upcoming projects in one AdminLTE UI.

It serves tech SMEs and agencies needing a unified quote-to-cash workflow. Unlike generic SaaS, it’s Laravel-native (self-hostable), integrates tightly from quotations to invoices, and will add project budgets and progress tracking.
