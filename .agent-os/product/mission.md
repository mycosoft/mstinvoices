# Product Mission

## Pitch

Invoice Generator is a company management platform for technology service companies that helps teams manage invoices, quotations, expenses, and (next) projects by providing a streamlined workflow from quotation to payment with clear reporting and an AdminLTE-powered UI.

## Users

### Primary Customers

- Tech Services SMEs: Manage client work, billing, and expenses
- Freelance/Agencies: Track quotes-to-invoice flow and payments

### User Personas

**Operations Manager** (28-45 years old)
- **Role:** Oversees company operations and billing
- **Context:** Coordinates invoicing, approvals, and expense control
- **Pain Points:** Manual tracking, overdue invoices, low visibility
- **Goals:** Faster billing cycles, accurate reports, centralized data

**Project Lead** (25-40 years old)
- **Role:** Manages client projects and deliverables
- **Context:** Needs to track project progress, costs, and profitability
- **Pain Points:** Disconnected tools, unclear budget status
- **Goals:** Real-time progress, budget adherence, easy reporting

## The Problem

### Disconnected billing and operations
Manual tools split quotes, invoices, and expenses across files leading to errors and delays; DSO increases and cash flow suffers.

**Our Solution:** Centralize the quote-to-cash flow with integrated expenses and reporting.

### Limited visibility into project costs
Teams lack a single source of truth for project budgets and spend, causing overspending and missed margins.

**Our Solution:** Project module linking tasks, time/costs, invoices, and payments.

## Differentiators

### Laravel-native, AdminLTE UX
Unlike generic SaaS, this runs on Laravel 12 with AdminLTE so teams can self-host and extend easily, resulting in faster iteration and lower TCO.

### Tight quote-to-invoice integration
Unlike siloed tools, quotations convert directly to invoices with status tracking, improving billing speed and accuracy.

## Key Features

### Core Features
- **Invoice Management:** Create, send, track status, and export PDFs
- **Quotation Management:** Convert to invoice; email templates
- **Expense Tracking:** Categorize expenses with approvals and reports
- **Client Directory:** Store contacts and company details
- **Reports & Analytics:** Revenue, cash flow, and status dashboards

### Upcoming Features
- **Project Management:** Projects with milestones, tasks, and budgets
- **Project Financials:** Link expenses and payments to projects
- **Project Progress:** Status, percent complete, and burndown
- **Time Tracking (optional):** Billable hours to invoices
